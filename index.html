Our Story

Built on
Quality & Purpose

We started as a passion project at a kitchen table, and while we've grown, we've stayed true to our roots. Today, Greater Than operates as a specialised home-based studio in Bedford, equipped with professional-grade technology. We've traded the "hobby" tools for industrial gear, but our "small business" heart hasn't changed. By keeping things personal, we can treat every single stitch and print with the same "perfectionist" eye we had on day one.

Vision:Setting the gold standard for custom apparel, from Bedford to the world.
Mission:Professional personalisation with a personal touch.
Our Promise

We don't believe you should have to order a hundred shirts just to get one you love. We've built our business around three core pillars:

No Minimums: Whether you need one bespoke gift for a friend or a full set of uniforms for your team, we're on it. No order is too small.
Premium Garments: We only use high-quality apparel that feels as good as it looks. If we wouldn't wear it ourselves, we won't sell it to you.
Hand-Finished Care: Every item is checked by a real person (not just a machine) to ensure your design stands the test of time.

Let's create something together. Whether you have a fully-vetted brand logo or just a rough idea on a napkin, we're here to help you bring it to life.

FAQ

Common Questions

What's your turnaround time?+

DTF printing: 3–5 working days. Custom embroidery: 7–10 working days. Rush options may be available — contact us to discuss.

Is there a minimum order for custom work?+

DTF printing has no minimum — we'll do a single piece. Embroidery requires a minimum of 6 garments. Promotional merch minimums vary by product.

Do you ship across the UK?+

Yes — we ship to all parts of the UK. Standard delivery 2–4 working days. Free shipping on Ready-to-Wear orders over £70.

Can I supply my own garments?+

Yes — we accept customer-supplied garments for printing and embroidery. Please contact us before sending to confirm compatibility.
